Chapter 01 · illustrative content

Greet someone respectfully

By the end of this chapter, greet an elder, teacher, or unfamiliar adult, ask how they are, and respond when they ask you back.

Chapter mission

Complete this short exchange with limited or no prompting:

నమస్కారం. బాగున్నారా?

బాగున్నాను. మీరు?
